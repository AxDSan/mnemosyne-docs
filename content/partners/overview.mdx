# Sponsors & Partners

Mnemosyne is MIT-licensed and self-funded, with in-kind support from companies that provide compute credits, hosting, and developer tooling. Those contributions pay for the things that are expensive to run in the open: nightly recall benchmarks, multi-model parity tests, and CI across the support matrix.

Every partnership is listed here and on [mnemosyne.site/partners](https://mnemosyne.site/partners). Nothing is silent.

<Callout type="info" title="Disclosure">
Compute Partners are disclosed material connections under the FTC Endorsement Guides (16 CFR Part 255). Sponsors have no editorial control over benchmark methodology, results, or technical claims.
</Callout>

## What we accept

- Compute credits (LLM, embedding, GPU inference)
- Hosting and infrastructure credits
- Developer tooling licenses and observability platforms

## What we do not accept

- Payment for feature prioritization or roadmap placement
- Payment for integration order in code or documentation
- Sponsorships from companies whose products conflict with our values (surveillance, privacy violations, vendor lock-in by design)
- Editorial veto over benchmark methodology or technical content

## Editorial control

The maintainers retain full control over benchmark methodology, results, and any technical claim made about a sponsor's product. If a sponsor's product has a real flaw surfaced by our benchmarks, we publish it. When we publish content about a sponsor or their product, we label it `#ad` or "Sponsored" per the FTC Endorsement Guides.

A partner listing is never a recommendation to use that provider over any other. Mnemosyne works with any OpenAI-compatible endpoint, and works fully offline with a local GGUF model and no provider at all.

## Termination

Either party can end a sponsorship at any time. On termination, we remove the partner's listing within 30 days.

## Partner with us

If your company runs an OSS credits program, open an issue on [GitHub](https://github.com/mnemosyne-oss/mnemosyne/issues) tagged `sponsorship`, or email `sponsors@mnemosyne.site`. We respond within a few business days.
